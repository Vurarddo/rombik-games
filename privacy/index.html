Privacy Policy — Rombik Games

Effective date: 19 November 2026

In short:
  • Rombik Games does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data — neither the child's nor the parent's.
  • No accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party data-collecting SDKs.
  • The app requests no device permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos).
  • The app itself does not connect to the internet. The one-time purchase is processed entirely by Apple or Google.
  • The few links out of the app (rate, share, write to us) sit in the parents' area behind a sum for grown-ups and open in other apps.

1. Who we are

This policy applies to the mobile app Rombik Games (listed in the App Store and on Google Play as “Rombik: Toddler Games” and shown as “Rombik” under its icon; bundle ID com.rombik.toddlersgame), published by Daria Kolpakova (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy, write to daka.kolp01@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no registration, no sign-in, no chat, no user-generated content that leaves the device, and no forms for entering a name, e-mail, age or any other personal detail.

If a parent writes to us, by e-mail from the parents' page or otherwise, we receive what the letter contains, such as the sender's address. We use it only to answer, never for marketing, never pass it on, and delete the correspondence on request.

3. Information stored only on your device

To remember your choices between launches, the app keeps a few settings in its private storage on the device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else:

SettingWhy
Day or night look of the app (switched by touching the sun or the moon)To keep the chosen appearance
Music and sounds on or offTo keep the chosen sound settings
App language (English, Ukrainian, German, Spanish, Polish or Russian)To keep the language chosen on the parents' page
Whether free colouring is on in the colouring gameSo the game opens in the same mode next time
Which pictures and things each game showed latelySo the next game shows different ones
Whether the full version has been purchasedTo keep the paid games open, even offline

Deleting the app removes all of this data.

4. In-app purchase

Some games are opened by a one-time in-app purchase. The purchase (and its restoration) is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own terms and privacy policies. We never receive or see your name, payment card, e-mail or other billing details; the app only learns from the store whether the purchase is owned. It asks again at launch and when the parents' page opens, so that on Google Play a refunded purchase closes the paid games again.

  • Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases can only be started from the parents' area, which is designed to be used by an adult. It opens only after a simple sum for grown-ups is answered, whether from the gear button on the meadow or from a locked game's house, and the purchase itself asks another sum before the store's payment sheet appears.

5. Advertising, analytics and third parties

The app shows no advertising, contains no analytics or crash-reporting services, does not track users across apps or websites, and does not share or sell any data to third parties. It contains no links to websites or social networks that a child could open. The only links out of the app sit in the parents' area, behind the grown-ups' sum, and none of them sends any data from the app:

  • Rate the app opens its own page in the App Store or on Google Play, governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policy.
  • Share the app opens the device's own share sheet with a short message and a link to our website. Nothing is sent unless the parent picks an app and a recipient; that app's own policy then applies. The link carries only the tag utm_source=app, which says where the link came from and nothing about who shared it.
  • Write to us opens the device's mail app with our address and a subject filled in. The letter is written and sent by the parent, and we use it only to reply. On a device without a mail app it opens our support page instead.

Our website, vurarddo.github.io/rombik-games, is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies, analytics or tracking; GitHub may keep technical logs of visits under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Children's privacy

Rombik Games is made for young children. Because we collect no personal information at all, we comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, including Article 8), the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection”, as well as the Apple Kids Category and Google Play Families requirements. No parental consent is needed, since no data is collected.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you. The app gives us no personal data, so the only thing we may hold is a letter you sent us yourself: ask, and we will tell you what we have or delete it. All on-device settings disappear when the app is removed. You may contact us with any question.

8. Security

Settings are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read, and no data is transmitted over the network by the app.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins to process data differently, we will update this page and the effective date above before such a version is released.
